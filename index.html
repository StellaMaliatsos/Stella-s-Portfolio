    About me

    Every company I've worked with has two versions of how work gets done: the one in the process documents, and the real one, full of workarounds, exceptions and knowledge that only lives in people's heads. I build products for the second one.

    AI runs through how I work, from LLM-assisted discovery synthesis to rapid prototyping with Claude Code and Lovable.
